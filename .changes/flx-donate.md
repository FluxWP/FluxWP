Flux Donate screen (`flxb-donate`): dashboard, donations list and the donation page (movable boxes, other plugins' boxes placed in, every action as a mutation) on the Flux GraphQL shell; donors, the donor card and recurring donations; the donation types list (order, open / close / archive, sample sets), the type editor, Add donation (the manager's form and the staff donation request) and Partners (list, settlement, editor).
