# Flux AI Chatbot, Live Chat & Customer Support

An AI chat window for your site. The assistant answers from your pages, posts, WooCommerce products and Flux Wiki through
**Flux AI & MCP**, and hands the chat to a live agent when needed. With **Flux Chat** installed, every site chat shows up in the
call center's single inbox next to WhatsApp (marked with a channel icon); otherwise the plugin's own inbox is used.

- Streaming answers, product cards (View / Add to cart), order status for logged-in customers, lead capture (Flux CRM).
- Modes: AI + live chat, AI only, live chat only. Working hours, "leave a message" outside hours, email replies.
- Queue position, typing indicator, read receipts, file sharing, end-of-chat rating.
- Fully customisable in the panel with a live preview: color (follows the theme by default), light/dark, header style,
  avatar, launcher icon and label, position, spacing, size, radius, home screen, suggested questions, contact links, teaser.
- Visibility rules: page types, guests/members, desktop/mobile, excluded paths.
- Open from anywhere: `[flx_chatbot]`, `#flxcb-open`, `data-flxcb-open`, `FluxChatbot.open()`.
- WooCommerce orders placed within 7 days after a chat are attributed to it.
- Privacy: no IP addresses stored, files kept outside public folders, retention period.
