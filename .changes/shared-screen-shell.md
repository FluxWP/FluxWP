`@fluxwp/ui`: new `Screen` (title, icon, subtitle, actions, notice, tabs; one place for padding, max width, safe area and overflow) and `Tabs` (Flux Admin style underline tabs, `size="sm"` pill variant). Module screens (admin, ads, ai, analytics, app settings, backup, blocks, chatbot, headless, languages, reCAPTCHA, roles, SEO, setup, social, speed, theme, wiki) use them instead of their own header/tab markup and CSS.
